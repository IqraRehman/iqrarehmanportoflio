    Questions people ask me

    Something else on your mind? Ask in the chat or message me on WhatsApp.

    Which platforms do you build on?

    WordPress (Elementor Pro, Divi, WPBakery, ACF), Shopify, WooCommerce and Webflow. I also hand-code with HTML, CSS and JavaScript when a builder isn't the right fit.

    How long does a website take?

    A landing page can be ready in 1 day. A standard business website usually takes 3 to 5 days, and an online store 5 to 8 days, depending on the number of pages and how ready the content is.

    Can you turn my Figma design into a website?

    Yes. I build Figma designs pixel-accurately in WordPress, Shopify, Webflow or custom code, responsive on every screen size.

    Will my website be fast and SEO-ready?

    Yes. Every site is built for Core Web Vitals and aims for 90+ PageSpeed scores, with clean structure, schema markup, a sitemap and proper indexing set up before launch.

    Do you work with international clients?

    Yes. I have worked remotely with clients and teams in Pakistan, Dubai, the UK, Germany and the US, and adjust meeting times to your time zone.

    Do you offer maintenance after launch?

    Yes. I offer ongoing maintenance covering updates, backups, security checks, uptime monitoring and content changes.
